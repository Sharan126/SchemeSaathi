## 2. Comprehensive Evidence Verification Matrix

### Scheme 1: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DA&FW).
- **Official Portal:** `https://pmkisan.gov.in`
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** Ministry Operational Guidelines (Feb 2019, amended 2023 for e-KYC/land seeding); Source Ref Date: `2019-02-01`.
- **Financial Benefit:** ₹6,000 per year, paid in three equal 4-monthly installments of ₹2,000 directly via DBT into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
- **Eligibility Predicates:** Cultivable landholding farmer family (husband, wife, minor children).
- **Mandatory Exclusions:** All institutional landholders; farmer families where any member holds/held constitutional posts, former/serving Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors, Chairpersons of District Panchayats; serving or retired government officers/employees; professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs); anyone who paid income tax in last assessment year; pensioners receiving ₹10,000+/month.
- **Mandatory Documents:** Land ownership records (RoR/Khatauni), Aadhaar card, Bank passbook linked with Aadhaar.
- **Application Channel:** PM-KISAN Portal (Self-registration) or Common Service Centres (CSC).

### Scheme 2: Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) — Version 2
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), National Health Authority (NHA).
- **Official Portal:** `https://pmjay.gov.in` / `https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in`
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** Union Cabinet Expansion Notification & PIB Release PRID 2053818 (Oct 29, 2024); Source Ref Date: `2024-10-29`.
- **Financial Benefit:** Cashless health cover up to ₹5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across 29,000+ empaneled hospitals.
- **October 2024 Senior Citizen Expansion (Vaya Vandana):**
  - **Age Criterion:** All Indian senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible **irrespective of socio-economic status or income**.
  - **SECC/BPL Independence:** SECC deprivation criteria do NOT apply to the 70+ demographic.
  - **Family Arrangement:** Senior citizens in families already covered under PM-JAY receive an exclusive, dedicated ₹5 Lakh top-up card reserved for the seniors.
  - **Private Insurance & Other Schemes:** Seniors with private health insurance or ESIC are fully eligible. Seniors under CGHS, ECHS, or CAPF may choose to either continue their existing scheme or switch to AB PM-JAY.
- **Mandatory Documents:** Aadhaar card (used strictly for offline age verification and e-KYC on the portal).
- **Application Channel:** NHA Beneficiary Portal or Ayushman App (Distinct **Ayushman Vaya Vandana Card**).

### Scheme 3: Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Women and Child Development (MWCD), Maternal and Child Nutrition Division.
- **Official Portal:** `https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in`
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** Section 4(b) of National Food Security Act, 2013 & PMMVY Mission Shakti Guidelines; Source Ref Date: `2017-01-01`.
- **Financial Benefit:**
  - First child: ₹5,000 in two installments (₹3,000 upon pregnancy registration + at least 1 ANC; ₹2,000 upon child birth registration & first cycle immunization).
  - Second child: ₹6,000 in a single installment, provided the second child is a girl child.
- **Eligibility Predicates:** Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM) aged 19 years and above.
- **Exclusions:** Women in regular employment with Central/State Governments or PSUs who receive paid maternity leave.
- **Mandatory Documents:** Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card, Aadhaar card of mother, Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
- **Application Channel:** Nearest Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or online via `pmmvy.wcd.gov.in`.

### Scheme 4: Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students (PMS-SC)
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE), Department of Social Justice.
- **Official Portal:** `https://scholarships.gov.in` (National Scholarship Portal)
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** Centrally Sponsored Scheme Revised Guidelines (Dec 2020); Source Ref Date: `2021-04-01`.
- **Financial Benefit:** Full compulsory non-refundable fees reimbursement + annual academic maintenance allowance ranging from ₹2,500 (Class 11/12) to ₹13,500 (Group 1: Medicine, Engineering, Ph.D.).
- **Eligibility Predicates:**
  - Citizen belongs to Scheduled Caste (SC) community.
  - Enrolled in recognized post-matriculation or post-secondary courses.
  - Total annual family income from all sources does not exceed ₹2,50,000 (₹2.5 Lakhs).
- **Mandatory Documents:** Caste Certificate issued by competent Revenue Authority, Income Certificate, Previous Year Marksheet, Fee Receipt, Aadhaar.
- **Application Channel:** National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or State Scholarship Portals.

### Scheme 5: Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs / National Savings Institute.
- **Official Portal:** `https://www.nsiindia.gov.in` / Department of Posts (`https://www.indiapost.gov.in`)
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** Government Savings Promotion Act, 1873 & Sukanya Samriddhi Account Rules, 2019 (GSR 914(E)); Source Ref Date: `2015-01-22`.
- **Financial Benefit:** Sovereign-guaranteed high interest rate (currently 8.2% p.a.), exempt under Section 80C with EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) tax status.
- **Account Limits & Rules:** Minimum annual deposit ₹250; maximum ₹1,50,000 per financial year. Deposits allowed for 15 years from account opening; maturity at 21 years or upon marriage after age 18.
- **Eligibility Predicates:** Girl child aged 10 years or younger at account opening. Maximum 2 accounts per family (exception for twins/triplets).
- **Mandatory Documents:** Birth Certificate of girl child, Identity and Address Proof of parent/guardian.
- **Application Channel:** Any Post Office branch or designated commercial bank branches.

### Scheme 6: Divyangjan Swavlamban Yojana
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD), National Handicapped Finance and Development Corporation (NHFDC).
- **Official Portal:** `https://nhfdc.nic.in`
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** DEPwD Concessional Credit Guidelines & RPwD Act 2016; Source Ref Date: `2018-04-01`.
- **Financial Benefit:** Concessional loans up to ₹50,00,000 (50 Lakhs) at low interest rates (5% to 8% p.a.) for self-employment, higher education, or assistive equipment purchase. Rebate of 1% for women with disabilities.
- **Eligibility Predicates:** Any Indian citizen with disability having 40% or more disability (certified under Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016), aged 18 years and above. No specific family income ceiling (concessional slabs based on project cost).
- **Mandatory Documents:** Unique Disability ID (UDID) Card / Disability Certificate issued by CMO, Project Proposal for loan, Aadhaar card.
- **Application Channel:** State Channelizing Agencies (SCAs) or designated partner banks.

### Scheme 7: Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Rural Development, National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP).
- **Official Portal:** `https://nsap.nic.in`
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** Article 41 of the Constitution of India & NSAP Guidelines 2014; Source Ref Date: `2014-04-01`.
- **Financial Benefit:**
  - Age 60 to 79: Central assistance of ₹200/month (States provide mandatory top-ups, typically raising total pension to ₹1,000–₹2,500/month).
  - Age 80 and above: Central assistance increases to ₹500/month.
- **Eligibility Predicates:** Age 60 years or older AND family must belong to Below Poverty Line (BPL) household according to SECC criteria.
- **Mandatory Documents:** Proof of Age (Voter ID, School leaving certificate), BPL Card / Ration Card, Bank passbook.
- **Application Channel:** Gram Panchayat office / Block Development Officer (Rural) or Municipality (Urban) and `nsap.nic.in`.

### Scheme 8: Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)
- **Ministry / Dept:** Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD), Rural Housing Division.
- **Official Portal:** `https://pmayg.nic.in`
- **Gazette / Statutory Reference:** PMAY-G Framework for Implementation (March 2016, extended to March 2029); Source Ref Date: `2016-04-01`.
- **Financial Benefit:** Unit assistance of ₹1,20,000 in plain areas and ₹1,30,000 in hilly/difficult/IAP areas for construction of disaster-resilient pucca house (minimum 25 sq. metres including hygienic cooking space) + ₹12,000 for toilet under SBM-G + 90/95 days of unskilled wage labor under MGNREGA.
- **Eligibility Predicates:** Rural families identified under SECC 2011 deprivation criteria living in kutcha or dilapidated houses, with no adult earning member or homeless.
- **Mandatory Exclusions:** Ownership of motorized 2/3/4-wheeler, mechanized 3-4 wheeler agricultural equipment, Kisan Credit Card with limit ₹50,000+, any household member as a government employee, monthly income exceeding ₹15,000, paying income tax.
- **Mandatory Documents:** SECC Household ID, Land / Site title, Aadhaar card, Bank account details.
- **Application Channel:** Gram Sabha beneficiary selection and Awaas+ portal verification.
